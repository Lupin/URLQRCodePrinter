URLQRCodePrinter

Collect links from your browser and keep them in collections, one per theme or project. They stay on your device, unless you ask for a shortened link yourself. The extension reads the address and title of the page you target, or the address of the link you target, and saves them. The app lays out what you print: sheets of sticky labels, a Niimbot printer over Bluetooth, or images to print elsewhere. No account to create, no server run by the publisher, and no analytics.

Features

  • Quick link capture

    Right-click a page or a link, or use the toolbar button. The page title is kept, so the label stays recognisable.

  • Managing collections

    Create as many collections as you need. A link you add goes into the one on screen. You can move it to another collection later, without it being created again.

  • Index each collection on its own

    Every collection has its own starting number: the first link takes it, and the next ones follow. Clearing the collection does not send it back to 1.

  • Lay out and print

    Four outputs: A4 or Letter label sheets, a printed table, a Niimbot printer, or images to print elsewhere. You pick a ready-made layout, or set the grid by hand. The preview shows the layout at the scale of the window: it is not a print proof.

  • Print directly

    To a Niimbot D110, M2 or M3 printer over Bluetooth, with no file in between.

  • Shorten links

    A shorter link gives a smaller QR Code. Shortening goes through an outside service: nothing leaves until you click, and the original address stays saved. Create a T.LY account (referral) credits the project if you follow that link; it changes nothing in what is sent.

  • Export

    CSV, Markdown, a JSON archive, a spreadsheet with the QR Codes, or PNG images in a ZIP archive. You take your data away in a readable format.

  • Import collection

    A JSON archive or a CSV exported from here reads back. The links return, with no duplicate. The import then asks whether to add to the collection on screen, replace it (name and note included), or file the links in a new collection.

  • No server

    The web app works on its own, with nothing to install. The extension adds collection from the browser.

Genesis of the extension

I built this extension for my own notebook. I keep my research and my finds on paper, and I used to copy the addresses into it by hand. Writing them took time, and finding them again took just as long.

I started by printing the QR Codes one at a time on a Niimbot. Each label took time, and went through a third-party app on my phone. The friction soon outweighed the benefit. Collecting and printing had to become faster and smoother.

I wanted collections so that links could be found again by theme and by project. They let you build reference pages, subject by subject, for a research notebook.

It is useful for what you want to keep within reach: an article, a product page, a video, a track or a record, a location on a map. You scan the label and find the link again, without copying it. You share it too: you hand over the label, and the link follows. And above all, a great deal of knowledge fits on one sheet: print a table of QR Codes, and you carry the whole collection away.

- Gaël A.G. -

How to use it

  1. Install the extension, or open the application in your browser.
  2. There are two ways to add a link. The extension button in the browser toolbar opens a window: the page title is already filled in, and one click on “Add this page” saves it. Right-click works too, on the page, on a specific link or on selected text.
  3. Choose the collection: the one on screen receives what you add. The application creates more, and “Move to…” files a link elsewhere afterwards.
  4. Open the application: your links are there, with search, sorting and collections.
  5. Tick the links you want to print, choose the layout, then click Print. The browser’s print window opens, with your printers.
  6. For a Niimbot printer, open the “Niimbot label” tab, connect it over Bluetooth, then print.
  7. To take your data away, use the exports: label images (ZIP archive), sheet or table (ZIP archive), a spreadsheet with the QR Codes, CSV, Markdown, a JSON archive. They are also how you print from another program.
  8. To read them back elsewhere, click “Import…” and give the JSON archive or the CSV exported from here. The import then asks whether to merge into the collection on screen, replace it, or file the links in a new collection; links already present never create a duplicate.

Shortening links and exports are options.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no publisher server, and no analytics.

Where are my links stored?

On your device, in the browser’s storage. They go nowhere unless you ask for a shortened link yourself.

Can I use it without the extension?

Yes. The application open in your browser lays out and prints. The extension adds collection from the browser: the toolbar button and the right-click menu.

Is shortening automatic?

No. Nothing is shortened until you click Shorten. The original address is always kept.

Which label printers are supported?

The Niimbot D110, M2 and M3, over Bluetooth. For the others, print the sheet on A4 label paper or on plain paper, or export the images.

Can I print on an ordinary printer?

Yes. You print the sheet or the table from the browser, on a sheet of sticky labels or on paper. To print from another program, export the images.

How do I move my links elsewhere?

Through the exports: CSV, Markdown, a JSON archive, or the PNG images in a ZIP archive.

How do I delete everything?

The “Clear collection” button erases the links in the collection on screen. Removing the extension also erases its local storage.

Is it free?

Yes. The code is open source, under the MIT licence.

Privacy

What is read. The address and title of the page you act on, and nothing else. Only at the moment you ask.

What is not done. No access to your history. No reading in the background. No account, no server run by the publisher. No analytics, no advertising, no selling of data. No code is loaded remotely: everything ships inside.

The only network request. If you ask for a link to be shortened, that address is sent to the service you chose: T.LY, TinyURL, is.gd, v.gd or spoo.me. Nothing is shortened by default. Without that click, no request is made.

What it does not do

  • Safari cannot print directly. Safari does not implement Web Bluetooth, and it imposes its engine on every browser on iPhone and iPad. Image export remains available everywhere.
  • Brave needs a manual step. Web Bluetooth is disabled by default there. It has to be enabled through the brave://flags/#brave-web-bluetooth-api flag, then Brave restarted.
  • Niimbot printing has only been tried on a D110. The M2 and M3 profiles exist, but printing on those machines has not been tested on real hardware.
  • The web app and the extension do not share data. The web app stores in the browser, the extension in its own storage. Use one or the other: export is the bridge.
  • Shortening depends on a third party. T.LY, TinyURL, is.gd, v.gd and spoo.me are outside services. If they close, a link already printed stops working. The original address stays in your collection and in your exports.
  • Not every browser is covered. The extension is published on the Chrome Web Store, for Chrome, Brave and Edge. No Firefox version is published. The Safari version is built from source and needs a few settings by hand, once. The web app stays usable with nothing installed.
